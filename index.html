📖 Role guide

Mafia

🕴️ Godfather — picks the nightly kill; looks innocent to Detectives.

🕵️ Consigliere — learns a player's exact role.

🔪 Mafia — takes over the kill if the Godfather dies.

Innocent

👤 Villager — no power.

💉 Doctor — protects one player each night.

🔎 Detective — checks if someone is suspicious.

🛡️ Bodyguard — dies in place of who they guard.

Neutral

🃏 Jester — wins if voted out.

🔪 Serial Killer — last one standing wins.

🌿 Survivor — just stay alive.

Alien

👽 Alien — abducts (kills) each night. Once per game, the whole team can Convert a player instead.

🌅 Some mornings a random event happens: rumors, tips, curfews, fire alarms and more.
