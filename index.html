📖 Role guide & how locations work

🌆 Locations: Hospital, Park, Shop, Police Station, Docks, Bank. Every night your role sends you (or lets you choose) a location. During the day, non-Mafia players pick 🗳️ Vote or 🔍 Investigate — investigating a location reveals real clues about who was seen there.

Mafia

🕴️ Godfather — picks the kill target and where it happens.

🕵️ Consigliere — learns a player's exact role each night.

🔪 Mafia — backs up the kill, or sabotages a location to hide evidence there.

Innocent

👤 Villager — visits a location each night; no other power.

💉 Doctor — saves one person from the night's attack.

🔎 Detective — learns if a player is suspicious.

🛡️ Bodyguard — dies in place of whoever they guard.

📹 Guard — checks a location's camera footage for visitors.

🕵️‍♂️ Tracker — follows a player to see where they went, even if cameras are down.

Neutral

🃏 Jester — wins alone if voted out by the town.

🔪 Serial Killer — kills alone; wins by being the last one standing.

🌿 Survivor — no team; just needs to make it to the end.

The exact mix depends on player count — bigger games unlock more roles.
